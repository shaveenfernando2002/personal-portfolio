I'm in the Co-op stream of Seneca's Computer Programming & Analysis diploma, on track to graduate in 2027. Most of what I build sits in the JavaScript ecosystem — React and Vite on the front end, Node and Express on the back, with PostgreSQL or MongoDB underneath.
